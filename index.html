Before you pack

Good to know, in plain words.

Passport and entry

Indian passport holders can enter Thailand visa-free for tourism. Your passport must be valid for at least six months from 22 November 2026.

Fill in the Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) online in the three days before you fly. We will send the link and help anyone who gets stuck. Carry a printout of your return ticket and hotel bookings.

Full Moon Party: stay safe, have fun

The party runs on Haad Rin beach, Koh Phangan, from sunset to sunrise. We travel there and back as one group, on our booked boat.

Wear neon or white, and closed sandals or old sneakers: the sand hides broken glass. Carry a little cash, your phone in a waterproof pouch and a photocopy of your passport. Leave the passport itself in the resort safe.

Agree a meeting point when we land. Stay with at least one friend, keep away from the fire-rope shows, and only drink what you have watched being poured. Thailand's drug laws are strict and penalties are severe.

Weather and sea

Late November is the rainy season on Koh Samui and Koh Phangan, with short heavy showers. Pack a light rain jacket and a dry bag.

Ferry and boat timings can shift with sea conditions. If that happens we will adjust the plan on the day and keep you posted on the group WhatsApp.

Flights and baggage

Thai Airways economy usually allows 30 kg check-in plus 7 kg cabin bag, and Bangkok Airways business allows more. Check the allowance printed on your e-ticket before packing.

On 26 November the Samui flight and the Chennai flight are separate tickets. Collect your bags at Bangkok, then check in again at the Thai Airways counter for TG 359.

Money and phones

Carry Thai baht in cash for the party, small shops and tips. Resorts and most restaurants take cards. A Thai tourist SIM or an international roaming pack is worth sorting out before you leave.

Meals and changes

All lunches and dinners are at pure vegetarian restaurants. Jain meals, with no onion, garlic or root vegetables, are arranged on request, so please tell us in advance.
